# Emergency Sinkhole Repair in Aurora, Indiana

**When disaster strikes, you need a team that responds fast.** That's exactly what happened when Aurora, Indiana homeowners discovered an 8-foot-deep sinkhole had swallowed their driveway overnight. Here's how Probst Excavating turned a homeowner's nightmare into a success story.

## The Problem: A Massive Sinkhole in Aurora

![Sinkhole damage in Aurora Indiana driveway](/images/blogs/aurora-sinkhole/sinkhole2.jpg)

Imagine waking up to find your driveway collapsed into an 8-foot-deep chasm. For this Aurora family, that's exactly what happened. The culprit? Decades-old drainage infrastructure that had finally given out.

When we arrived on-site, we discovered a dangerous mix of:
- **Old galvanized pipes** - Corroded and failing
- **Concrete culvert sections** - Cracked and misaligned
- **Critical utility lines** - Main electric, gas, and water lines running directly overhead
- **Unstable ground** - The collapse compromised the entire driveway structure

This wasn't just a cosmetic issue—it was a safety hazard that needed immediate attention.

## Our Solution: Complete Culvert Replacement

### The Probst Excavating Approach

**Safety First:** With major utility lines crossing the work area, precision was critical. We carefully mapped and protected all electric, gas, and water lines to the house before excavation began.

**Complete Removal:** We didn't just patch the problem—we eliminated it. The entire mishmash of failed galvanized and concrete culvert pipes was excavated and removed.

## Why Sinkholes Happen in Aurora & Lawrenceburg

The Tri-State area's soil composition and weather patterns create unique challenges:

### Common Causes in Southeast Indiana:

1. **Aging Infrastructure** - Many properties in Aurora, Lawrenceburg, and surrounding Dearborn County communities have drainage systems installed 30-50+ years ago
2. **Freeze-Thaw Cycles** - Indiana winters are hard on underground pipes
3. **Soil Erosion** - Water flowing through compromised pipes washes away soil
4. **Mixed Materials** - Older repairs often used whatever was available, leading to mismatched connections that fail over time

## Warning Signs of Drainage Problems

Don't wait for a collapse! Watch for these red flags:

- **Depressions in your driveway or yard** - Small dips can become big problems
- **Standing water** after rain near driveways or culverts
- **Cracking pavement** - Often indicates movement below
- **Eroding soil** around pipe openings
- **Water flowing where it shouldn't** during storms
